What “750” inside your ring actually means

Hallmarks are the oldest consumer protection law there is. Here is how to read the tiny stamps inside your jewellery.

Look inside any gold ring we make and you will find a row of tiny stamps. They are not decoration. They are a legal guarantee, struck after an independent assay office has tested the metal.

The fineness mark

750 means 750 parts in 1,000 are pure gold, which is 18 carat. 585 is 14 carat, 925 is sterling silver and 950 is platinum. If the number is there, the metal has been tested and proved.

The maker's mark

Ours is AS in an oval. It tells you, and anyone who owns the piece after you, who made it. We register it with the assay office like a signature.

Next time you are buying second-hand, look for all three: the fineness, the maker and the office mark. Together they are a passport for the piece.
